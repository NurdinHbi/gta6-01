HOW TO PLAY

On foot: WASD to walk, Shift to run. Drag the mouse to orbit the camera.
Cars: walk up to any car and press E to get in. Parked cars are free to take — the law notices. W/S accelerate and brake, A/D steer, Space is the handbrake, E gets out.
Combat: F punches. Click fires your pistol. Hitting people and shooting raises your wanted level.
Wanted: foot cops chase from 1 star, patrol cars join from 2. Lay low and the heat fades — or get Busted or Wasted and lose 10% of your cash.
Missions: follow the gold beacon. Mara pays $300, $500, $800 and $1,500 across four jobs. Finish them all to own the city.
Save: progress autosaves. Continue from the title screen.
