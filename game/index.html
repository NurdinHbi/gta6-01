SOLMAR CITY

Sun. Sea. Speed. And a fixer named Mara.

Eight jobs. One coastal city. About 150 buildings, a beach, a pier, traffic, pedestrians, police who do not forgive, and a helicopter that never blinks. Steal a car, lose the heat, get paid.

An original game for the make.fun gta6-01 bounty. No network needed — it runs entirely from these files. Enter or click New Game to start.

HOW TO PLAY

W A S DMove / drive (W accelerate, S brake & reverse)
ShiftRun on foot
Mouse dragOrbit camera · C toggles chase cam while driving
SpacePunch on foot · handbrake while driving
F / clickShoot · 1/2/3 weapons · R reload
EEnter / exit a car or boat
P / EscPause · M mute

Follow the yellow beacon and the minimap marker. Stealing cars, hitting pedestrians and shooting in public raise your wanted level: foot cops chase at 1 star, cruisers at 2, roadblocks and a helicopter at 4. Lose them by breaking line of sight and staying quiet. Busted or Wasted costs 10% of your cash.

Between jobs: 10 gold briefcases are hidden around the city ($250 each) and 3 stunt ramps pay $600 for a clean landing. Progress saves automatically.
